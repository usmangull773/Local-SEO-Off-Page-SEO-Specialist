01

What we found

Your brief names the real problem clearly: national reach with a local profile, and a strategy that has to serve both without confusing either. Three things follow from that.

Local and national intent are competing for the same signals

High impact

When Houston service terms and national terms sit on the same pages and in the same posts, Google gets mixed relevance signals. The GBP should own service plus Houston. National pages should own everything else.

Houston GBP and local pages

  • permit-ready commercial building plans Houston
  • commercial architect Houston
  • site feasibility study Houston
KEEP APART

National content

  • permit-ready construction documents
  • commercial architectural design services
  • architectural visualization for developers

GBP posts need to lead with the developer's problem

Medium impact

Developers and GCs read posts to judge whether you understand their schedule risk. A post that opens on a keyword or a service list loses them in the first line.

High impact

1

Build and verify both keyword sets

Service plus Houston terms for the profile, national terms for content. Every volume cross-checked in Semrush and Ahrefs, with a written reason for each priority.

Why: nothing gets built on a number that cannot be explained.
2

Rebuild the Google Business Profile

Categories, services, products, attributes and Q&A aligned to the local set. Baseline grid scan in Local Falcon so movement is measured from day one.

Why: the profile is the only asset that ranks in the Houston local pack.
3

Fix NAP and citations

Audit every directory and citation for name, address and phone consistency, then correct or claim.

Why: inconsistent NAP weakens local trust signals.
4

Audit links, then build relevant ones

Toxic link review and disavow where warranted, anchor distribution planned, then editorial links from architecture, construction and CRE sites, plus AEC directories and associations.

Why: relevance of the referring site matters more than volume.
5

Expert commentary from your principal architect

Journalist and trade publication pitches on permitting, feasibility and development topics, with your principal as the named source.

Why: it earns editorial links a directory never will.
6

Weekly report, including what did not work

Local pack movement, organic positions, every link with live URL, domain metrics and anchor, and GBP engagement.

Why: you check the work, not take it on trust.
